# Hospital Demo Guide

## Quick Summary

Open the demo here:

**https://hospital-demo-frontend.onrender.com/**

This demo shows how a home health organization can manage public information, patients, appointments, medical records, prescriptions, lab results, and staff workflows in one place.

### Public features

Visitors can view:

- Home and organization information
- Home health team information
- Healthcare services
- Doctor profiles
- Career information
- Contact information
- Light and dark themes

### Patient features

Patients can:

- View and update their profile
- Request appointments
- Review medical records
- Upload and download documents
- View prescriptions and request refills
- Review lab results
- Add family members

### Staff features

Staff can:

- Review and update appointments
- View patient records
- Add clinical notes
- Control whether notes are staff-only or shared
- Review lab orders and results
- Review billing information
- View email activity

## Detailed Instructions

### 1. Open the website

Open a modern browser and go to:

**https://hospital-demo-frontend.onrender.com/**

The first page may take a few seconds to appear if the hosted service has been inactive.

### 2. Explore the public pages

No account is needed for this part.

1. Start on **Home** to see the organization overview.
2. Open **About Us** to learn about the organization.
3. Open **Our Team** to learn about the people who provide and coordinate care. Team members can use **Team Login** on that page.
4. Open **Services** to review the available healthcare services.
5. Open **Doctors** to browse the doctor directory.
6. Select a doctor to view their specialty and details.
7. Open **Career** for employment information.
8. Open **Contact Us** to see contact information.
9. Use the sun/moon button to switch between light and dark mode.

### 3. Sign in as a patient

1. Choose **Login** in the navigation bar.
2. Enter `patient@example.com`.
3. Enter `Pass1234!`.
4. Choose **Sign in**.
5. Open the patient profile area.

### 4. Review the patient profile

Open **My Profile**.

Here you can review the patient information and update available profile details. To demonstrate the update feature:

1. Change one non-sensitive field.
2. Save the change.
3. Refresh the page.
4. Confirm that the change remains.

### 5. Request an appointment

Open the appointments area.

1. Review any existing appointments.
2. Choose the option to request an appointment.
3. Select a doctor.
4. Choose a date and time.
5. Enter a reason for the visit.
6. Submit the request.
7. Return to the appointment list and confirm that it appears.

### 6. Review medical records

Open **Medical Records**.

Review the available sections for:

- Medical history
- Allergies
- Current medications
- Shared clinical notes
- Patient documents

To demonstrate document handling:

1. Select the upload option.
2. Choose a small PDF or image.
3. Submit the file.
4. Confirm that it appears in the document list.
5. Download the document.

### 7. Review prescriptions

Open **Prescriptions**.

Review the listed medications, including dosage, frequency, pharmacy, and refill information.

To request a refill:

1. Find an active prescription.
2. Choose **Request Refill**.
3. Confirm the request.
4. Check the refill request status.

### 8. Review lab results

Open **Lab Results**.

1. Review the available lab orders.
2. Select a completed order.
3. Review the individual test values.
4. Look for reference ranges and abnormal-result flags.

### 9. Add a family member

Open **Family Members**.

1. Choose **Add Family Member**.
2. Enter a name, such as `Jane Doe`.
3. Enter a date of birth.
4. Select a relationship.
5. Choose any available permissions.
6. Save the family member.
7. Confirm that the new family member appears.

### 10. Switch to the staff account

Choose **Logout**, then choose **Login** again.

Use:

- Email: `staff@example.com`
- Password: `StaffPass123!`

### 11. Review the staff dashboard

Open **Staff Dashboard**.

Use it to review appointments across patients. Select an appointment to review its details and update its status when appropriate.

### 12. Review a patient record

From the staff dashboard, open a patient record.

Review the patient's:

- Profile information
- Medical history
- Allergies
- Medications
- Notes
- Documents

### 13. Demonstrate note visibility

From the patient record:

1. Add a clinical note.
2. Mark it as staff-only.
3. Confirm that it appears in the staff view.
4. Change it to shared with the patient when available.
5. Sign in as the patient.
6. Open Medical Records.
7. Confirm that the shared note is visible.

### 14. Review staff tools

The staff navigation includes these areas:

- **Lab Results**: review and manage lab information
- **Billing**: review bill information
- **Email Logs**: review appointment-related email activity

These pages are useful for showing how staff manage information behind the patient-facing portal.


Use fictional information only. This is a demonstration site, not a production medical records system.
